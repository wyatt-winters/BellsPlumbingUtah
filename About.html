Who We Are

Bells Plumbing is a locally owned and operated plumbing company providing expert drain cleaning, sewer repair, water heater services, leak detection, and full plumbing solutions to homes and businesses across Utah.

Founded and led by Jeremy Bell, Bells Plumbing was built on a simple idea: deliver honest, high-quality plumbing work at fair prices — no corporate pressure, no unnecessary upsells, just real craftsmanship you can count on.

Jeremy's hands-on approach and deep commitment to customer satisfaction are the foundation of everything we do. When you call Bells Plumbing, you're getting a team that treats your home like their own.
