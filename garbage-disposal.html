Repair or Replace That Disposal? Here's How to Know.

If your disposal leaks from the bottom housing, is more than 10 years old, or has tripped the reset button repeatedly, replacement is usually cheaper and quieter than chasing one repair after another.

Signs you need a new disposal, not just a repair:

  • Leak is coming from the bottom of the unit (housing crack)
  • Disposal is 10+ years old and noisy
  • Foul smells coming from the disposal
  • Motor hums but blades won't move even after unjamming
  • Rust visible on the underside or grinding chamber
