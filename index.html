Emergency & Leaks

Burst pipe, flooding, no water? Same-day response, 24/7.

Common signs you need this

  • Burst pipes or major active leaks
  • No water anywhere in the house
  • Sewage backing up indoors
  • Water heater leaking onto the floor

What we do

24/7 emergency response across Davis, Weber, Salt Lake, and Box Elder counties. We stop the active leak first, then give you a flat-rate price in writing before any repair work begins.

Why Bells

No upcharge for after-hours calls. Same licensed plumber start to finish — not a rotating dispatcher.

Sewage coming up? We diagnose with a camera, then clear it fast.

Common signs you need this

  • Multiple drains slow at once
  • Gurgling toilets or tubs
  • Foul smell near floor drains
  • Water/sewage backing up in the basement

What we do

We start with a sewer camera so we know exactly where the blockage or break is — no exploratory digging. Then we clear with hydro jetting or repair the line as needed.

Why Bells

You see the camera footage with us before we recommend anything. We don't push full line replacements when a targeted repair will do.

Thaw, repair, and prevent — before a freeze turns into a burst.

Common signs you need this

  • Faucets running at a trickle or not at all
  • Frost on visible pipe runs
  • Knocking or banging sounds inside walls
  • A burst is suspected behind a wall

What we do

Safe controlled thawing (no torch-on-PEX shortcuts), then a leak check on every joint that was affected. We can also add foam insulation, heat tape, or freeze-resistant fixtures to high-risk runs.

Why Bells

Utah winters are our home turf. We know which lines freeze first in this climate and how to keep them from doing it again.

Find hidden leaks fast with no unnecessary damage to walls or floors.

Common signs you need this

  • Water bill spiked with no obvious cause
  • Damp spots on a wall, ceiling, or floor
  • Sound of running water when nothing is on
  • Warm spots on the floor (slab leak)

What we do

Acoustic sensors, thermal imaging, and pressure testing to pinpoint the leak BEFORE we cut anything open. Small access hole, fast repair, minimal drywall work afterward.

Why Bells

We charge a small diagnostic fee that's applied toward your repair if you have us do the work.

Warm spots, mystery bills? We pinpoint and repair leaks under the slab.

Common signs you need this

  • Warm or wet spots on floor tiles
  • Unexplained jump in water bill
  • Sound of running water when no fixtures are on
  • Loose tiles or buckling flooring

What we do

Electronic and acoustic detection narrow the leak to a specific spot under the concrete. We then choose the least disruptive fix — spot repair through a small access cut, or rerouting the line through the wall above.

Why Bells

We never just cut the slab open hoping to find it. Every fix starts with a confirmed location.

Wet yard or low pressure? We locate and repair water main breaks.

Common signs you need this

  • Soggy patch in the yard
  • Sudden drop in water pressure
  • Discolored water at the tap
  • Higher water bill with no usage change

What we do

We locate the break with leak-detection equipment, then choose between spot repair, partial replacement, or trenchless replacement of the full line. Curb-to-house mains usually take a single day.

Why Bells

Licensed and bonded for water main work in Utah. Call (385) 255-8400 for an on-site quote.

Drains & Sewer

Slow drain, clogged sink? Cleared fast with same-day service.

Common signs you need this

  • Sink, tub, or shower drains slowly
  • Standing water in the tub
  • Gurgling sound after the water drains
  • Bad smell rising from the drain

What we do

For routine clogs we snake the line; for grease, scale, or repeat clogs we recommend hydro jetting to scour the pipe. Either way you get a flat-rate price before we start.

Why Bells

If a basic snake won't fix it long-term, we'll tell you straight — not push a service you don't need.

High-pressure cleaning for grease, sludge, and tree roots.

Common signs you need this

  • Drains keep clogging shortly after a snake
  • Multiple slow drains at the same time
  • Tree-root intrusion confirmed on camera
  • Heavy grease buildup in kitchen lines

What we do

Up to 4,000 PSI of pressurized water scours the pipe walls clean — not just punching a hole through the clog like a snake does. We camera-inspect before and after so you see the difference.

Why Bells

When done correctly, jetting can extend the life of older lines years past where they'd otherwise need replacement.

Real-time HD video down the line — no guessing, no demolition.

Common signs you need this

  • Recurring sewer backups
  • About to buy or sell a home
  • Tree roots suspected in the line
  • Older home (1970s or earlier) with original lines

What we do

A high-resolution camera runs the full length of your line so you can see exactly what's going on — cracks, roots, separations, bellies, or blockages. We record the video and walk you through it on the spot.

Why Bells

Every recommendation we make after a camera inspection comes with the footage to back it up. No vague "it's probably bad" diagnoses.

Roots, cracks, or collapse — diagnosed with a camera, then repaired.

Common signs you need this

  • Multiple drains in the house backing up
  • Confirmed root intrusion on camera
  • Cracks, separations, or a collapsed section
  • Bellies (low spots) holding water in the line

What we do

After we know exactly what's wrong from the camera inspection, we choose between spot dig + repair, full open-trench replacement, or trenchless (pipe bursting / lining) depending on the line's condition and your yard.

Why Bells

You always see the camera footage and get an itemized quote BEFORE any digging happens.

Pipe bursting and lining — repair the line without tearing up the yard.

Common signs you need this

  • Long line run under landscaping, driveway, or patio
  • Mature trees you don't want to lose
  • Older clay or Orangeburg pipe
  • You want a long-term fix, not a band-aid

What we do

For pipe bursting, a new HDPE line is pulled through the old one, breaking the old apart underground. For lining, an epoxy-coated liner cures inside the existing pipe. Both methods leave your yard nearly untouched.

Why Bells

Not every job is a candidate for trenchless. We'll be honest about whether your line qualifies before quoting.

Recurring slow drains? Fix the cause, not just the symptom.

Common signs you need this

  • Same drain clogs again every few months
  • Smell coming back even after cleaning
  • P-trap or fixture rocking or leaking
  • Vent line issues (gurgling, slow drainage)

What we do

We treat recurring drain issues as a diagnostic problem first. Camera the line, identify the actual cause (venting, slope, buildup, root intrusion), and fix the source so you stop calling us back every quarter.

Why Bells

A single targeted repair almost always beats four years of repeat drain cleanings.

FAQ

Common questions before you call

How fast can you get to me for a sewer backup?

For active sewer backups we aim to be on-site the same day — often within 60 to 90 minutes during business hours. We treat backups as emergencies because the longer waste sits in the line, the more damage it can do.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. For most jobs we give you a flat-rate price in writing before any work begins. For diagnosis-heavy work like leak detection or sewer camera inspections there's a small service charge that's applied toward the repair if you book.

Are you licensed and insured?

Fully licensed and insured by the State of Utah. We're also current on manufacturer certifications for the hydronic, boiler, and water heater brands we install.

What is hydro jetting and do I need it?

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of your pipes — it cleans out grease, sludge, scale, and tree roots that a basic snake just punches a hole through. If your drains keep clogging or you've had a backup, jetting is usually the right fix.

Do you serve my area of Utah?

We cover Davis, Weber, Box Elder, and the northern Salt Lake County area — from Brigham City down through Bountiful. Scroll up to "Service Areas" for the full list, or just call and ask.

What does a service call cost?

We don't charge an inflated dispatch fee. For most calls you'll see a small diagnostic charge that's credited toward the work if you have us do the repair — and you always get a flat price in writing before we start.

Do you handle sewer backups and main line clogs?

Yes — that's one of our specialties. We diagnose with a sewer camera first so you know exactly what's happening in the line, then clear it with hydro jetting, snaking, or main line repair as needed.
