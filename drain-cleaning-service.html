Snaking vs. Hydro Jetting: What's the Difference?

Basic Drain Snaking

  • ✗ Punches hole through clog
  • ✗ Leaves buildup on walls
  • ✗ Recurring clogs common
  • ✓ Good for first-time clogs
  • ✓ $150–$350

Hydro Jetting (Better)

  • ✓ Completely clears pipe
  • ✓ Removes all buildup & grease
  • ✓ Prevents future clogs
  • ✓ Works for recurring problems
  • ✓ $350–$800 (permanent fix)

Bathroom sink, tub, or shower slow to drain
